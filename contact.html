FAQ

risevana's small class sizes are designed around genuine connection. Children in classes of 8 to 12 students form real friendships. Many families also tell us that risevana helped their child find a peer group for the first time, especially those who had previously felt out of place in a large school.

risevana follows a rigorous, internationally recognised curriculum. Teachers are qualified, experienced, and focused on the children in front of them rather than managing a class of 30. The Open House is a good opportunity to ask about academic outcomes directly.

Every child who joins risevana has a Family Success contact. If something is not working, a real conversation happens quickly. You are not navigating a system on your own.

Many of risevana's students joined from traditional schools. The transition is structured and supported. The Open House will give you a realistic sense of what that process looks like.

Sessions are kept to 30 minutes out of respect for your schedule, but the team is available to follow up individually if you want a longer conversation. Register and see what is possible.
